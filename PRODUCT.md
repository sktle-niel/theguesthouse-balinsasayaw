# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS (plus small vanilla JavaScript for the booking form and gallery), no build step, no database, no backend. Deployed by uploading files to Hostinger shared hosting (`public_html`).

## Users

Travellers to Puerto Princesa, Palawan, mostly on a phone, looking for a clean, affordable, air-conditioned room in the city before or after island tours. They want to see the rooms, know where it is, and reserve dates quickly.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and reservation site for The Guesthouse by Balinsasayaw. Success: a visitor sees the real rooms, trusts the place, and sends a booking request (dates, guests, room type, contact) to the guesthouse by email or SMS.

## Positioning

A small guesthouse connected to Balinsasayaw, whose over-the-water nipa-hut restaurant is part of the experience. Rooms are simple, clean and homey: white linens, hardwood furniture, local art.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Room types visible in photos: Twin rooms (two single beds), Single room (daybed), Double / Queen rooms. Official room names are not confirmed.
- No prices on the site (owner decision). Rates are given on inquiry.
- Amenities visible in photos: air-conditioning, fresh linens and towels, bottled water, mini fridge and kettle in some rooms, wardrobe, bedside lamps. Do not claim Wi-Fi, parking, pool, breakfast or check-in times. These are unconfirmed.
- Booking form must work with no backend: validate inputs, compute nights, show a summary, then open email or SMS.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "The Guesthouse by Balinsasayaw".
- Visual reference pinned by the owner: the "Horizon Bay Resort" landing-page concept (airy sky-blue hero fading to white, clean sans display, pill navigation, rounded arch-shaped room cards, circular amenity icons, centered section headings).
- Hero image: the over-the-water nipa huts of the Balinsasayaw restaurant.

## Product Principles

1. Show the real rooms honestly; no photo or claim that sets a wrong expectation.
2. Booking in under a minute on a phone.
3. Contact details always one tap away.
4. Light pages: fast on mobile data in Palawan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first. Readable contrast over photos, labelled form fields, keyboard-usable gallery and form.
