The Guesthouse

Tucked along PEO Road in Bancao-Bancao, The Guesthouse is a calm, clean place to rest between island days, with dinner waiting at the Balinsasayaw huts on the water.

  • Air-conditioned rooms
  • Fresh linens & towels
  • Bottled water
  • Fridge & kettle in select rooms
  • Restaurant over the water

Rooms to come home to

White linens, hardwood furniture and local art. Air-conditioned, quiet and kept spotless.
